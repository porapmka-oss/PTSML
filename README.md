## 🎯 Aim  
To study and implement the concept of **Sampling Distribution** using Python and verify the **Central Limit Theorem (CLT)** through simulation.

---

## 📖 Description  
This project demonstrates how sampling distribution works by:

- Generating a population dataset  
- Drawing multiple random samples  
- Calculating sample means  
- Visualizing the distribution of sample means  
- Comparing population mean with sampling mean  

---

## 🧠 Concepts Covered  

- Population and Sample  
- Sample Mean  
- Sampling Distribution of Mean  
- Standard Error  
- Central Limit Theorem  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies  

- Python  
- NumPy  
- Matplotlib

## ⚙️ Procedure  

1. Generate a large population dataset.  
2. Select multiple random samples from the population.  
3. Compute the mean for each sample.  
4. Plot histogram of all sample means.  
5. Compare results with population mean.  

---

## 📊 Expected Output  

- Histogram showing approximate normal distribution.  
- Mean of sample means nearly equal to population mean.  
- Verification of Central Limit Theorem.
